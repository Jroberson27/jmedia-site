Service Overview

Ongoing short-form video and photo content for local and regional businesses. Instead of a single shoot, this is a monthly content partnership: I show up on a set schedule, capture your business in action, and deliver edited short-form video and photos ready to post.

Who this is for: Businesses that know they need to show up online every month but do not have time to plan, shoot, and edit their own content. Same production standard I bring to theme park and broadcast work, applied to your brand on a recurring basis.

What's Included

  • Short-form video content, monthly

  • Photo content for each session

  • Guaranteed editing turnaround at higher tiers

How It Starts

Step one: A pilot session. One location, one session, 2 to 3 finished short-form videos plus a handful of photos. This is a content sample fee that covers time and gear, not a discounted retainer.

Step two: If it is a fit, move into one of three monthly retainer tiers below, sized to how much content your business needs each month.
