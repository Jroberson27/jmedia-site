The model

Why we work monthly

Most companies do not have a video problem. They have a volume problem.

You need a campaign this quarter, social cutdowns every week, something for the trade show, a customer story for the sales team. Handled as separate projects, every one of them starts over. New brief, new crew, new explanation of what you do and how you sound.

That is where the budget goes. Not into the work. Into the restarting.

A monthly partnership removes that. I learn your brand once. After that every shoot is faster, every edit sounds more like you, and you stop paying for the ramp up.
