Origin

Why JMEDIA exists

After years on professional productions I kept seeing the same problem. Brands needed good content consistently, and the traditional production model made that hard. Every project meant finding someone new, explaining the brand again, and starting the planning from scratch.

JMEDIA exists to give a business the creative expertise of a production team with the flexibility of a long term partner. Fifteen years of commercial and broadcast work, applied to your calendar instead of one shoot at a time.
