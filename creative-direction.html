Service Overview

Before I picked up a camera full time, I spent a decade in enterprise sales, including a run as a founding sales hire in hospitality tech. That background shapes how I approach a shoot. I am not just there to point a camera, I am thinking about who the content is for, what it needs to do, and how it fits the bigger picture of your brand.

Where this shows up: Pre-production planning, shot lists built around what will actually convert, on-set decisions that keep the brand consistent, and post-production choices that serve the goal, not just the aesthetic.

Where I Can Help

  • Pre-production planning and shot lists

  • Brand consistent visual strategy

  • Content aligned to a business goal

The Sales Background

Why it matters: Sales taught me how to read a room, understand what actually moves a customer, and communicate a value proposition clearly. On a shoot, that translates into content built to perform, not just look good.

Hospitality experience: Years spent as a founding sales hire in hospitality tech gave me a close look at how hotels and resorts think about guest experience and brand presentation, a lens I bring to every shoot in that space.
