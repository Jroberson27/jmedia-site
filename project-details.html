Let Fear Consume You: HHN 2026

On-set camera work for Universal Orlando's Halloween Horror Nights promotional campaign. This project is a representative example of the on-set and broadcast work that makes up most of my working hours, theme park productions built to move fast and hit a hard release date.

My role on this production was camera operator, working within a larger crew to capture promotional content for one of Universal Orlando's biggest annual events. The finished piece was cut for social and broadcast distribution.

On This Shoot

Theme park productions like this one run on tight schedules and require camera operators who already know the workflow: hitting marks, working around park operations, and adapting quickly when a shot list changes on the day.

This is the same standard of on-set discipline I bring to every production, whether it is a theme park campaign, a national brand commercial, or a monthly content partnership for a local business.
